What’s being tested?

A fixed trend rule uses seven earlier completed price observations and elapsed time to estimate the price at your chosen horizon. It buys only when the estimated upside clears the modeled trading costs.

Positions close at the next decision, then the rule reassesses. A falling forecast keeps cash; it does not open a short.

Data, costs & limits

Replay uses actual Coinbase minute closes from October 10–11, 2026. Missing shared minutes are skipped. Replay bid/ask is assumed at 2 bps each side of the close.

Live mode uses public Coinbase bid/ask snapshots, polled every 10 seconds. Buys fill at ask plus slippage; sells fill at bid minus slippage, with fees on both sides. Account value includes hypothetical exit costs.

Live sessions run only while this tab is open. Pauses and connection gaps are recorded; there are no invented trades between observations. Forecasts settle on the first available later quote; lateness is shown.

The earlier trend test did not establish an edge. This is an experimental rule, not a trained or validated trading model. Longer horizons extrapolate a short recent trend. A profitable replay does not establish reliable future profit.
