What’s being tested?

The trend rule uses the configured number of earlier completed observations and actual elapsed time. It buys only when its forecast clears modeled trading costs and the entry buffer.

Positions close at each decision, then the rule reassesses. Stops also check observed frames. Falling forecasts keep cash; this account is long only, with no leverage. Optional account limits close positions and block new entries after a drawdown or daily loss breach.

Data, costs & limits

The included Coinbase sample needs no new market download. A date-range replay fetches public completed minute closes, with causal warmup excluded from scored results. Missing shared minutes are skipped. Replay bid/ask is assumed, not archived.

Live bid/ask is polled every 10 seconds. Local HTTP receipt is availability time; the exchange timestamp is the last trade’s time. Buys use ask plus slippage, sells use bid minus slippage, and both pay fees. Values include exit costs.

Live sessions run only while this tab is visible. Gaps do not create intervening trades. Outcomes more than 30 seconds late expire and are excluded from accuracy. A replay closes on its exact selected final frame.

Repeatedly changing settings after seeing results can overfit the data. This is an experimental rule with no established edge. A profitable replay does not prove future profit. Browser-local records and hashes are not a tamper-proof audit.
